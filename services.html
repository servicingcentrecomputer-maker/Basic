How it works

A simple process, made to fit.

No black boxes. You'll always know what's happening, what's next, and what I need from you.

01

Listen

A relaxed kickoff to understand your business, your audience, and what success looks like. Then I gather your words, images, and any brand assets.

02

Shape

We agree on the sitemap and content structure. I sketch the key pages and define a clear visual direction before any code is written.

03

Build

Design and development happen together. You get a staging link to follow along, with regular check-ins and room for small course corrections.

04

Launch

We test every page and breakpoint, polish the details, and hand over clean source files. Then I stick around for questions after launch.
